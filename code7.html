The Art of Beautiful Web Typography

Typography is the visual component of the written word. It helps readers engage with content by making text both legible and appealing. Modern web design relies heavily on font selection, weight variation, and proper alignment to ensure balance and readability.

In digital media, designers often mix serif and sans-serif fonts to create hierarchy and contrast. For instance, headings might use a bold sans-serif typeface, while paragraphs stay light and spacious with a gentle line height. Proper letter-spacing ensures the text breathes and feels natural.

Ultimately, great typography is invisible — readers should focus on the message, not the font. Using consistent styles and spacing across a webpage gives a sense of professionalism and clarity.
